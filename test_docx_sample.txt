EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made between TechCorp Inc. ("Company") and John Smith ("Employee") effective January 1, 2024.

POSITION AND DUTIES
Employee will serve as Senior Software Engineer and will perform duties as assigned by the Company.

COMPENSATION
Base Salary: $120,000 per year
Performance Bonus: Discretionary annual bonus based on company performance
Benefits: Health insurance, dental coverage, 401(k) plan, 15 days vacation

TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. Company may terminate immediately for cause.

CONFIDENTIALITY
Employee agrees to maintain confidentiality of all company information indefinitely.

NON-COMPETE
Employee agrees not to work for competitors within 50 miles for 12 months after termination.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work products created during employment belong to the Company.

This agreement is governed by Delaware law.
