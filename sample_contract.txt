EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on January 15, 2024, between TechCorp Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Company"), and John Smith ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Employee agrees to serve as Senior Software Engineer and will perform duties as assigned by the Company. Employee will work full-time and devote best efforts to the Company's business.

2. COMPENSATION
Company shall pay Employee a base salary of $120,000 per year, payable in bi-weekly installments. Employee is eligible for annual performance bonuses at Company's discretion.

3. BENEFITS
Employee shall be entitled to participate in Company's health insurance plan, dental coverage, and 401(k) retirement plan. Employee receives 15 days of paid vacation annually.

4. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. Company may terminate immediately for cause, including violation of company policies, poor performance, or misconduct.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employee agrees not to disclose any confidential information obtained during employment. This obligation continues indefinitely after termination.

6. NON-COMPETE
Employee agrees not to work for direct competitors within 50 miles of Company headquarters for 12 months after termination.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work products, inventions, and ideas created during employment belong to the Company. Employee assigns all rights to such intellectual property.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by Delaware law. Any disputes will be resolved through binding arbitration.

By signing below, both parties agree to the terms of this Employment Agreement.
